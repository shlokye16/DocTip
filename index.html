Before starting: obtain appropriate consent from everyone being recorded/listened to and follow your institution’s privacy policies. This browser demo uses speech recognition to convert nearby speech into text and sends recognized text to the local demo server; it does not intentionally store or upload raw audio. Speaker identification is not supported, so the transcript may mix clinician and patient speech. Review the transcript and prompts; do not enter real patient information.
